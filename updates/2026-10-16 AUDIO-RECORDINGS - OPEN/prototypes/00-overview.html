Audio recordings — the design language

One calm horizontal player bar, everywhere audio lives. Played against the real 19-minute call. Flip the theme (top-right). Each block notes the anatomy, the token used, and the motion. This is the foundation every surface agent builds on — see design-spec.md.

1 · The bar — anatomy

Left→right: play · waveform (scrub + progress) · time · transcript icon · ⋯ menu. Height 56px, radius 10 (rounded-lg), surface-sunken fill, 1px border. The play button is the only accent-filled element on the bar.

play 36px, radius 6, accent fill waveform flex, 32px tall, 2px bars / 1px gap time mono 12px, tabular transcript 28px ghost, accent when present ⋯ 28px ghost gap 12px · pad 8/12
played (accent) playhead (accent-glow) unplayed (text-muted / .35)

2 · The full state machine

Every state is quiet and honest — background work never blocks. Absence of a transcript is healthy, not an error. Every state shares one bar height (56px standard · 44px compact) — recording, saving and broken/missing are normalized, never taller; the quiet status line for recording/saving/transcribing sits below the bar, leaving the bar itself unchanged.

3 · Responsive widths

The bar is fluid: the waveform absorbs the width, controls stay fixed. Below ~360px the time readout drops to current-only; the compact variant (gallery tile / search hit) trims height to 44px.

4 · Inline in a note

Audio is media. It sits in the writing flow exactly like an image MediaBlock — left-aligned, breathing room above/below, never shouting.

5 · Transcript reading — docked player + word highlight

Player docked on top (sticky, Spotify-like). Words highlight one-by-one as audio plays; click any word to seek there. Press play and watch. Speakers are grouped and told apart by weight/tone — never a second colour. The transcript is always shown polished (no raw/polished toggle, no meta chips). This same reading component serves both presentations below.

6 · Desktop split view

A transcript opens in two presentations, both from the ⋯ menu: this split view — the default, which the transcript-icon click also opens — with the note editor on the left and the transcript (text + docked player) on the right, drag the divider to rebalance; and Open transcript — reader, a focused full overlay (player docked on top, transcript below) for distraction-free reading.
