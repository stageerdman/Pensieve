Pensieve · Phone concepts

Three magical spines for the phone. Capture a thought, summon a memory. Open each full-screen on your iPhone.

  • Chosen · v2
    The Wand
    The locked direction, round 2: rock-solid reel, tap a memory → full screen, Arc-style tabs (Cards), autosave, chrome hides while typing, left-thumb action strip, and the desktop Accio + Sync icons. Sync: tap to cast, hold for details.
  • 01 · v1
    The Wand (first pass)
    The original capture-first concept — kept for reference / comparison.
  • 02
    The Reel
    Recall-first. The home is the flask reel — the centre flask magnified, neighbours shrinking on a current. Accio re-filters the same surface, so browsing and searching are one.
  • 03
    The Pool · Basin
    The most magical. A living pool of floating flasks. Pull up to pour a new thought, pull down to summon, and sync ripples across the water as a spell.
All three share Pensieve's world — the Basin's blue-black stone, the magical-blue accent, gold, and the glass flasks. Pick what to keep; the next round narrows to the winner.
