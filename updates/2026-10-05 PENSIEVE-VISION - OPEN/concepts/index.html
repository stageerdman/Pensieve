The Pensieve, reimagined

Four concept directions for what capturing and revisiting memories could look and feel like if the app truly became the Pensieve. Each opens as a live, interactive mockup.

Concept 01
The Basin
A stone chamber with a living silver pool. Pour a thought in and watch it spiral down; browse memory-wisps drifting on the surface (time as depth) and dive into one for an immersive read.
Open →
Concept 02
Strands & Vials
Dumbledore's memory cabinet: thoughts become coiling silver strands sealed in labelled vials on lit shelves. Uncork one and the strand unspools into the memory. Search & category shelves.
Open →
Concept 03
The Memory Constellation
A star-field of memories you drift through; note-links are glowing threads, the ones you revisit shine brightest. Click a star to read it and ignite its connections. An era dial pulls focus across the years.
Open →
Concept 04
Everyday Pensieve
The pragmatic one: a fast, calm daily app that keeps the Pensieve soul as atmosphere. A "pour a thought" bar, a shimmering memory stream, an AI coach that resurfaces the past, and a focus "dive" reader.
Open →
