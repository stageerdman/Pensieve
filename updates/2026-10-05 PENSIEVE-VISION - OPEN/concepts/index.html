The Pensieve, reimagined

Four concept directions for what capturing and revisiting memories could look and feel like if the app truly became the Pensieve. Each opens as a live, interactive mockup.

Concept 01
The Basin
A stone chamber with a living silver pool. Pour a thought in and watch it spiral down; browse memory-wisps drifting on the surface (time as depth) and dive into one for an immersive read.

Concept 02
Strands & Vials
Dumbledore's memory cabinet: thoughts become coiling silver strands sealed in labelled vials on lit shelves. Uncork one and the strand unspools into the memory. Search & category shelves.

Concept 03
The Memory Constellation
A star-field of memories you drift through; note-links are glowing threads, the ones you revisit shine brightest. Click a star to read it and ignite its connections. An era dial pulls focus across the years.

Concept 04
Everyday Pensieve
The pragmatic one: a fast, calm daily app that keeps the Pensieve soul as atmosphere. A "pour a thought" bar, a shimmering memory stream, an AI coach that resurfaces the past, and a focus "dive" reader.

Round two · Quick orientation

Fusing what you loved — the Basin's magic, Everyday's practicality, and the glass flasks of Strands & Vials — into one question: the best way to glance and know your most important thoughts. Each uses categories, tags, recency, pins & revisits as real intelligence.

Orientation 01
The Surface — importance rises
A calm pool where the thoughts that matter rise as the brightest, nearest flasks. Near/bright = matters now; a tiny trace says why ("pinned · revisited 11× · 3d"). Steerable weight sliders; "Stir" resurfaces important-but-forgotten.
Open →
Orientation 02
The Mind Map
A zoomable map where categories are fixed regions you learn by heart; importance = size + glow + nearness to centre. Tag chips physically regroup matching flasks into a bright ring — find and prioritise in one motion.
Open →
Orientation 03
Summon — Accio for thoughts
A command ring: the empty state is already your most important thoughts. Keyboard-first chips (category, #tag, pinned, important) stack live; matches fly in on silver trails. Near-zero-latency targeted find.
Open →
Orientation 04
The Apothecary — shelves at a glance
The Vials cabinet re-engineered for density: a persistent "Important" top shelf, category shelves below, importance encoded in each flask's size/brightness/fog. Facet rail filters all shelves at once. Scan dozens, the important jump out.

Round three · One behaviour for every overview

The real activities — a working set (pins), topical shelves you make on demand (@Sales Mindset), and weekly→monthly reviews — all powered by one scalable idea: a shelf / basket is a saved lens over your flasks. Two expressions of it.

Behaviour 01
Shelves — the universal container
Every shelf is the same object: a saved lens. Working Set = lens "pinned"; @Sales Mindset = lens "tag"; This Week = lens "date range". Conjure one in a gesture. Reviews = step through a time-shelf, then seal a review flask that rolls up by date. The pragmatic skeleton.
Open →
Behaviour 02
The Memory Desk — the enchanted bench
The same basket=lens idea, made tactile and fun: flasks on a warm bench under the brooding basin. The weekly review is a ritual — the basin replays your week, untouched flasks gather dust, a forgotten memory resurfaces. The soul.
