One idea runs this whole screen: a shelf is a saved LENS over your flasks.
Your Working Set is just the shelf whose lens is "pinned". A topic like @Sales Mindset is a shelf whose lens is a tag. This Week is a shelf whose lens is a date range. Nothing here is a bespoke feature — they are all the same object. Conjure a shelf in one gesture (pin a tag · pick dates · write a rule) and keep it as long as it's useful. A review isn't new either: it's simply stepping through a time-shelf — and sealing a weekly review mints its own flask that rolls up into This Month. Inside every shelf the importance engine sets rank, size and brightness (near & bright = matters now) and always tells you why.
