Forms · UX concept round · 2026-11-12

Six teams, eighteen concepts

Every file is clickable and built on the real app's stylesheets. Each concept covers five surfaces: A template builder · B desktop fill · C phone capture · D after sync, where AI fills answers · E weekly compress. Each file has its own light/dark toggle.

Where the teams agree (unprompted)

  • Three field types are enough (5 of 6 teams): Question (answer shape: paragraph, bullets or numbered, plus optional guide text), Scale (1–N with an anchor on each point), and Checklist. Freeform, the list answers, "failed → lesson" and Mr Nice Guy all become a Question. Bret Victor's team splits List out as a fourth type.
  • Phone "One Take" with chapter marks (5 of 6 teams): record once and tap Next per question. The desktop then knows exactly which words answer which question, with no AI on the phone.
  • AI writes in a second ink (all 6 teams): it never overwrites your words or your taps. Editing AI text makes it yours. Weekly compress skips any field you're typing in.
  • The empty new memory offers your forms and the offer disappears once you type. A template is written like a normal note, not in a separate builder.
