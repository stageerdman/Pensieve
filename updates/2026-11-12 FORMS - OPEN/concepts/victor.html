See what the form knows

A form shouldn't be a dead document you fill once. It's a live connection between a question, the raw thing you said or shot, and every day you've answered it. So nothing is hidden: you edit the template and watch every past form respond; you scrub through the recording and watch the answers follow; you point at any word the AI wrote and see the exact sentence it came from. No accept/reject dialogs. AI text is just text that keeps a link to its source until you make it your own.

Immediate connectionEvery edit shows its effect straight away: template, scale anchors, the boundaries between recordings.
Show the dataEvery field shows its own history. The 1–5 scale shows your last 7 days as dots before you tap.
Make AI legibleTinted text = AI, plain = you. Hover it to see its source. Edit it and the tint goes, because now it's yours.
Nothing modalNo "review AI changes" step. Processing writes into the memory; you just keep writing over it.

Recommended field set: four answer shapes, plus guidance on any of them

The owner's nine Daily Review questions fit these four shapes with nothing left over. Freeform = Write with no prompt. Numbered = a List toggle. Failed → lesson = a List whose prompt contains "→". Every bullet then gets a "→" slot. It's a typing convention, not a fifth type. Guided prompt (Mr Nice Guy) = Write + a guide note. Guidance is a property that any shape can have, not a separate type.
