# WOW-SHOWCASE — the experience script

Who reads this: Jony (look and motion), Eraser (build), Columbo (flow and words).
The plan and the tests are in `update v1.md`. This file covers what the visitor sees, does and feels.

---

## 1. The person and the true job

**Who arrives:** someone curious, on a laptop, with about two minutes. They have voice memos
they never played back, a paper notebook they never reread and photos of whiteboards they
can't find. Their worry, which they rarely say out loud: *"I think good thoughts, then lose
them. Even the ones I keep, I never use."*

**What Pensieve really does for them:** it catches a thought in whatever shape it arrives
(spoken, written, filmed, on paper), and later every shape answers to one question.
That last part is the surprise nobody expects. A voice memo from Tuesday's walk and a paper
page from Monday night can be *asked* together.

So the showcase has one job: **let them catch thoughts in different shapes, then show
those shapes answering together.** The whole script builds to that last chat answer.

## 2. The world (and why this one)

Ideas I tested for "catch something fleeting, keep it, come back to it wiser":

| Source | Why it was tempting | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A firefly jar at dusk | catching small lights, one jar | Pretty, but the jar is the end. Nothing happens to fireflies later. **Cut.** |
| Bees and honey | many flowers (modes) → the hive turns nectar to honey (transcripts) → nourishment (insight) | The mapping is very good, but it would add a second world on top of the app's own. **Backup B.** |
| Message in a bottle, shore | phone throws, Mac receives | Bottles that drift are *lost*, the opposite of the promise. **Cut.** |
| A herbarium (pressed flowers, labelled) | capture + labels | Quiet and dead. The memories never speak back. **Cut.** |
| Rivers into one lake | many sources, one body of water | Good for the sync. Weak for capture. Use only the *pour*. **Kept as the sync motion.** |
| **The app's own world: Wand → bottles → basin** | Phone = the Wand, spells cast by one gesture; each memory is a glowing Flask; the Mac is where they pour together; Patronus answers | Needs no explaining, it's the real product. Every action maps 1:1 to something the app really does. **Recommended.** |

**Recommended world: "Catch it in any shape. Pour it in. Ask."**
- **Action** = casting a spell from the Wand (the real ring menu).
- **Success** = a bottle is corked and set on the shelf under the phone. One bottle per
  memory. The bottle's *glyph* shows its shape (mic, page, camera…).
- **Waiting** = bottles on the shelf glow faintly: caught, not yet understood. They are
  "empty light", with no words inside yet.
- **Coming back** = the MacBook. The bottles pour in, fill with words and become one basin
  you can ask questions.
- **Mistake** = none possible. Every mode has Show me, and you can skip anything. Skipped
  shapes arrive on the Mac as "from earlier this week", so the ending never breaks.

**Backup B, the hive:** captures are "gathering", bottles are "cells", sync "makes honey".
Use it only if the owner wants the showcase to live outside the app's own visual language.

## 3. Stage layout

```
 ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
 │  Pensieve                                          ◌ sound off  ⟲    │
 │                                                                      │
 │   ┌ guide ──────────┐   ┌──────┐                 ┌──────────────┐    │
 │   │ RECORD          │   │phone │      · · · ·    │   MacBook    │    │
 │   │ Walking, and an │   │      │                 │   (closed,   │    │
 │   │ idea arrives.   │   │      │                 │  faint glow) │    │
 │   │ ① Hold the wand │   │      │                 └──────────────┘    │
 │   │ ② Tap Record    │   └──────┘                 Open it when you're  │
 │   │ ③ Just talk     │   ▢ ▢ ▣ ▢ ▢ ▢ ▢ ▢ ▢   ← shelf  ready.         │
 │   │   [ Show me ]   │                                                │
 │   └─────────────────┘   Write Record Film Snap Pages iPad Form ✦ Accio│
 └──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```
- The **mode strip** under the shelf is how you jump between modes. A caught mode gets a
  filled dot.
- The **guide** always sits beside the phone, on the side away from the Mac.
- Narrow screens: the devices stack (phone on top, Mac below) and the guide becomes a
  sheet under the phone.

## 4. Scene order

| # | Scene | ~Time | What it's for |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | **Arrive** | 4 s | Calm. One line, two devices, nothing to learn yet. |
| 1 | **The Wand** | 6 s | The only gesture you need: hold → the ring blooms. |
| 2–10 | **Capture modes** (Write · Record · Film · Snap · Pages · iPad · Form · Patronus · Accio) | 8–15 s each | Catch the same week of thoughts in nine shapes. |
| 11 | **Pour** (click the MacBook) | ≤ 8 s | Everything arrives and fills with words. |
| 12 | **Ask** | open-ended | Talk to Patronus. The last answer weaves the shapes together. |
| 13 | **Rest** | — | One closing line and a quiet "Start again". |

The visitor may open the Mac at **any** time. After 3 caught modes the lid starts to glow a
little brighter (an invitation, never a nag). Idle for 20 s, and the stage plays the tour by
itself with the ghost finger. Any touch hands control back where it is.

### Scene 0 — Arrive
- Dark Basin background (dark theme by default; light works too). Phone lit, MacBook lid
  closed with a faint gold sync rune on its lid.
- Headline: **"Catch a thought in any shape."** Sub-line, fading in after a beat:
  *"Then ask it something, on your Mac."*
- Guide: *"This is a week in someone's life. Try catching it."* Button: **Begin**.

### Scene 1 — The Wand (wow #1: the bloom)
- Guide title: **THE WAND**
- Guide says: *"Everything starts with one button. Hold it."*
  1. Hold the wand button
  2. The spells bloom around your thumb
  3. Let go on the one you want
- Animation: the ghost finger presses the round button at the bottom of the phone. The
  screen blurs and darkens, and the ring of spell glyphs blooms out from the thumb (the
  real SpellRing look: Write, Accio, Sync, Snap, Record, Film, Journal, Form). One tiny
  twinkle as it opens.
- Done when the ring has bloomed once (by hand or Show me). No bottle, because this teaches
  the gesture and catches nothing.

## 5. The capture modes

The made-up week: someone who took up pottery and is wondering whether to teach a small
Saturday class. Every line below is invented. One thread runs through all nine shapes:
**"start before I feel ready."**

Each guide has the same frame:
```
 SPELL NAME                 (2xs caps, muted)
 The moment, in one line.   (base)
 ① step  ② step  ③ step     (sm, current step lit in accent)
 [ Show me ]                (one button; the phone does it)
```
When a mode is finished, its bottle drops onto the shelf. The guide shows one quiet line,
e.g. *"Caught. Saved on this phone."*, then offers **Next: Film →**.

### 2 · Write (journal, typing)
- **Moment:** *"Waiting for the kettle, a thought you want to keep."*
  1. Hold the wand, cast **Write**
  2. Type. There's no save button
  3. Tap back. It's kept
- **Animation:** the ring blooms. Write casts with its **gold sparkle** (the real cast).
  A fresh page slides up with the keyboard. The ghost types, at a human pace:
  > **Small rule for this week**
  > Start before I feel ready. Ready is a feeling that shows up *after*.
- **End:** the page folds down into a bottle with the pen glyph. *"Caught. Saved on this phone."*

### 3 · Record (speech, voice)
- **Moment:** *"Walking. An idea you'd lose by the next corner."*
  1. Cast **Record**
  2. Just talk, as long as you like
  3. Tap stop. Your Mac will write it down
- **Animation:** a full-screen recorder. A live waveform breathes with the made-up voice
  (a soft recorded voice, captioned), and the timer counts. Under the waveform a quiet line
  says *"Words arrive on your Mac."* The line the voice says (captions show it):
  > "I keep waiting to be good enough to teach. But the people who taught me weren't
  > masters. They were two steps ahead, and kind."
- **Honest note:** the phone does **not** show words appearing. That happens on the Mac in
  scene 11 (wow #5). The guide promises it here, so the Mac pays it off.
- **End:** the waveform shrinks into a bottle with the mic glyph.

### 4 · Film (video)
- **Moment:** *"Your hands are busy. Film it and talk over it."*
  1. Cast **Film**
  2. Your phone's own camera opens
  3. Record. It comes back as a memory
- **Animation:** the phone's camera view: a (drawn, not filmed) wobbly bowl turning on a
  wheel, with a red rec dot. Caption of the voice: *"Third try. Still wobbly. Still mine."*
  The clip is 8 s.
- **End:** a film-strip frame closes into a bottle with the video glyph.

### 5 · Snap (photo)
- **Moment:** *"The plan is on a sticky note. Don't retype it, take a photo."*
  1. Cast **Snap**
  2. Take the photo
  3. It's a memory. Your Mac reads the words in it
- **Animation:** shutter blink. The photo is a drawn sticky note in handwriting:
  *"Class idea: 4 Saturdays · 6 people · bring nothing"*. It lands as a photo memory.
- **End:** a bottle with the camera glyph.

### 6 · Journal pages (paper)
- **Moment:** *"You still write on paper. Bring a whole week in at once."*
  1. Cast **Journal**
  2. Turn the pages. The camera stays open
  3. Done. Your Mac sorts them into days
- **Animation:** the continuous page camera (the real Journal session). A paper page slides
  under the lens, *click*, the next page, *click*. A small page stack counter grows: 1, 2.
  The pages (handwritten, drawn):
  > **Mon 18** · Fear list: 1. nobody signs up. 2. someone signs up.
  > **Tue 19** · Asked the café about the back room. A yes before I'd finished asking.
- **End:** the stack becomes one bottle with the book glyph and a tiny "2" on it: one batch
  that will turn into two days on the Mac (wow #3, paid off in the pour).

### 7 · iPad + Pencil (handwriting)
- **Moment:** *"On the iPad? Write with the Pencil. It becomes type."*
  1. The same Wand, on iPad
  2. Cast **Write**, then write with Apple Pencil
  3. Your handwriting turns into text as you go
- **Animation (wow #4):** the phone **grows** into an iPad (one smooth resize, about 400 ms,
  then it sits wider on the stage). A pencil writes *"lip thinner, foot wider"* next to a
  small bowl sketch. The handwritten words melt into typed text a beat later.
- **Honest note (small, in the guide):** *"Handwriting to text is iPad's own Scribble."*
- **End:** the iPad shrinks back to the phone. A bottle with the pen glyph and a small
  pencil tip.

### 8 · Form (guided reflection)
- **Moment:** *"End of the day. Three questions, answered out loud."*
  1. Cast **Form**, then pick *Daily review*
  2. One question per screen. Talk through it
  3. Swipe on. It's one recording
- **Animation:** three cards, one at a time, over a single running recording:
  *What went well?* → *What scared you?* → *One step for tomorrow?*
  The captions answer briefly: *"The bowl held."* · *"Saying 'class' out loud."* ·
  *"Price it. Just a number."*
- **End:** a sealed package, a bottle with the form glyph.

### 9 · Patronus spell (a task for the Mac)
- **Moment:** *"Leave a job for later, then walk away."*
  1. Open a memory (the walk recording), then cast **Patronus**
  2. Say or type what you want done
  3. It waits. Your Mac casts it on the next sync
- **Animation:** the walk memory opens. The Patronus block appears at the end with its orb
  dimmed. The trigger line is in caps: **WHEN SYNCED · PATRONUS CASTS**. The ghost types:
  *"Turn this into three next steps."* Status: *"Waits for the computer."*
- **End:** no new bottle, because it isn't a new memory. The mic bottle on the shelf gets a
  small dimmed orb on its cork. On the Mac it lights up (paid off in the pour).

### 10 · Accio (find anything, on the phone)
- **Moment:** *"Find it in a second, whatever shape it was."*
  1. Cast **Accio**
  2. Type a word
  3. Every shape that matches comes up
- **Animation:** type *"class"*. The results come up in shape order: the sticky-note photo, the
  form, the walk memo (by title). Each row has its glyph. On the shelf, the matching bottles
  brighten for a moment, so the shelf and the search speak the same language.
- **End:** no bottle. The mode dot fills.

**Not a mode: Sync.** On the phone, Sync is a tap. In the showcase, the MacBook *is* the
sync, so there's no separate Sync mode to learn.

## 6. Scene 11 — The pour (click the MacBook)

The visitor clicks the closed MacBook (or **Open your Mac** in the guide).

1. **Lid opens** (about 500 ms). The real desktop home gallery is behind it, dim and empty.
2. **The thread** (wow #5): a thin gold line of light draws from the phone to the Mac, the
   same gold as the sync rune. The shelf bottles lift one by one along it, in the order they
   were caught (about 150 ms apart).
3. **Each bottle lands in the gallery and fills.** Each one arrives as empty light, and its
   liquid rises as its words appear beside it. Per shape:
   - **Voice (wow #6, the live transcription):** the walk memo opens big for a moment. The
     voice plays (if sound is on; captions either way), and the transcript *types itself in
     time with it*, word by word, with timestamps in the margin (`00:04`, `00:11`). This is
     the one slow moment, about 3 s, and it's worth it. Every other bottle fills fast.
   - **Film:** same, faster, with the clip's two lines appearing under a still frame.
   - **Photo:** the sticky note's handwriting gets a soft scan line, and the typed words
     appear under it.
   - **Journal pages (wow #3):** the "2" bottle *splits into two* labelled **Mon 18** and
     **Tue 19**. One batch of paper becomes two dated days.
   - **Write / iPad:** already words, so they fill instantly. That's a small, true point.
   - **Form:** fills as one memory with three answered questions.
   - **Patronus:** the dimmed orb on the walk memo lights up and breathes. The memory now
     ends with **Cast** and three made-up steps: *1. Write the price. 2. Ask the café for a
     Saturday. 3. Tell two friends.* (and an **Undo**, as in the real block).
4. **Skipped modes** arrive last, in a smaller group labelled *"From earlier this week"*,
   already full. The ending works whatever the visitor did.
5. A quiet status line at the bottom: *"9 memories · all words ready · saved on this Mac"*.
   Then the Patronus panel slides open with the cursor in the field.

Reduced motion: no thread or flight. The bottles appear in place already full, the
transcript appears whole, and it takes 2 s at most.

## 7. Scene 12 — Ask Patronus (wow #7: the shapes answer together)

Patronus opens with one line: *"I've read this week. Ask me anything about it."*
Three suggested questions, as chips (one tap each):

1. **"What keeps stopping me from teaching?"**
   > Mostly one fear: that you're not good enough *yet*. You said it on your walk
   > [🎙 Walk · 00:04], and your paper list names the other fear too, that someone *will* sign
   > up [📖 Mon 18]. In your review you called saying "class" out loud the scary part
   > [▤ Daily review].
2. **"What have I already done toward it?"**
   > More than it feels like. The café said yes to the back room [📖 Tue 19]. The plan
   > exists, 4 Saturdays for 6 people [📷 Sticky note]. And you've kept going at the wheel
   > [🎬 Third try].
3. **"Give me one step for this weekend."**  ← the closing answer
   > Put a number on it. Your own next step from last night was "price it" [▤ Daily review].
   > The plan is already on your sticky note [📷 Sticky note], and the café is ready
   > [📖 Tue 19]. Then ask for one Saturday. You already know you don't need to be a master,
   > just two steps ahead [🎙 Walk · 00:11]. You wrote the rule yourself: start before you
   > feel ready [✎ Small rule].

- **Citations are chips with the shape's glyph.** Hovering a chip lights its bottle in the
  gallery. Clicking it opens that memory with the line lit, and voice/video jump to the
  timestamp and play that line.
- The closing answer cites **five shapes** (form, photo, paper, voice, typed) on purpose.
  That's the whole product in one reply.
- **Free typing works:** any question gets the closest scripted answer. If none is close,
  Patronus says *"In this demo I only know this one made-up week. Try one of these."*
  and shows the chips again. It never invents claims about the visitor.
- Answers stream in at a reading pace (fast, about 40 chars per frame), never letter-by-letter slow.

## 8. Scene 13 — Rest
After the closing answer, the stage dims slightly and one line appears under the Mac:
**"Catch it anywhere. Understand it on your Mac."**, with a small text link: *Start again*.
No confetti, no score.

## 9. Small ideas, from inside the world, and the filter

| # | Idea | Kept? | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Each bottle on the shelf gets its shape's glyph | ✅ | One look tells you what the shelf holds. This is the core language |
| 2 | Bottles wait as "empty light" and fill with liquid as words arrive | ✅ | Gives "transcribed" a visible meaning. Reuses the Flask fill |
| 3 | Glass *tink* when a bottle lands, one note higher per bottle, so a full shelf plays a small scale | ✅ | Progress you can hear. It varies, so it never grows stale. Off by default, captioned visually |
| 4 | Journal "2" bottle splits into two dated days on the Mac | ✅ | Shows date detection and day splitting without one word of explanation |
| 5 | Phone grows into an iPad, then shrinks back | ✅ | Device change in one motion, no second stage |
| 6 | Gold thread phone → Mac during the pour | ✅ | Same gold as the real sync rune. Says "these are connected" |
| 7 | Voice transcript types itself in time with the voice | ✅ | The live transcription moment, placed where it's true |
| 8 | Hovering a citation lights its bottle | ✅ | Connects the answer back to the thing you caught yourself. Cheap |
| 9 | Patronus orb dimmed on the phone, lit on the Mac | ✅ | Shows "conditional spell" plainly, as in the real block |
| 10 | Lid glows a little brighter after 3 caught modes | ✅ | A quiet invitation, never a nag |
| 11 | Mode dots in the strip fill as you catch | ✅ | Wayfinding. The shelf alone doesn't show Accio/Patronus |
| 12 | Ghost finger self-plays the tour after 20 s idle | ✅ | Works on a booth screen or a portfolio page left open |
| 13 | Day/night: the Basin background slowly shifts from evening to morning over the tour | ❌ | Lovely, but means nothing about the product and costs paint |
| 14 | Confetti or "All caught!" badge when the shelf is full | ❌ | Gamey. Breaks the calm-notebook vibe |
| 15 | A narrator voice reading each guide | ❌ | Slows people down. The captions are enough |
| 16 | Typing sounds for Write | ❌ | Annoying the 100th time. The visual typing is enough |
| 17 | The bottle wobbles when you hover it on the shelf | ❌ | Decoration. Doesn't tell you anything |
| 18 | A 3D rotating MacBook | ❌ | Heavy, and it adds nothing to "open it" |
| 19 | Showing the visitor's own name in Patronus answers | ❌ | We don't know it. Faking it would be creepy |
| 20 | Live words on the phone while recording | ❌ | Not true of the real app (see Decisions in `update v1.md`) |

## 10. Sound (optional, off by default)
- One switch top right: *sound off / on*. Every sound has a visual twin, so nothing
  depends on it.
- Only three sounds: the shelf **tink** (rising), a soft **pour** for the thread (under 1 s),
  and the made-up **voices** for Record, Film and Form (captions always shown).

## 11. Accessibility and speed rules (non-negotiable)
- Everything works by keyboard: Tab to the mode strip, Enter for Show me, Space to hold
  the Wand (press = hold).
- `prefers-reduced-motion`: no bloom, flight, thread or splitting animation. States change
  in place, and every end state is still reached.
- Guide text is real text (screen readers read it). The devices are labelled regions.
  Each step's live status is announced politely.
- No magic waits on the visitor: Show me never takes longer than 6 s, and any click
  skips the current animation to its end.
