Open question #3 — where does a recording get born?

Two ways capture could start. I built the first and recommend it; the second is sketched so you can choose. Both end at the identical settled bar — the difference is only where the recording lives while it's happening.

● my default — built above

Capture starts as the inline bar

Selecting Audio in the slash menu drops the bar in place and it immediately becomes the live recorder. No surface change, no modal — you record exactly where the audio will live.

  • In-flow. The note never leaves your eyes; the bar you record into is the bar you'll play.
  • One component. Recorder and player are the same .audio-bar, just different states — honours "one bar, everywhere."
  • Calm. No overlay to dismiss; stop → save → settle happens under the cursor.
  • Tension: a long recording means a live bar sitting in the note while you keep typing above it (fine — it's quiet).
alternative — sketch only

A dedicated recorder that drops a bar on stop

Audio opens a small focused recorder (its own surface). You record there; on stop it inserts a settled bar at the cursor. Closer to a phone capture screen.
