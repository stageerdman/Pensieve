Audio inside a gallery — desktop

The gallery is a justified horizontal strip: you drag a container height, that becomes the shared row height, and every image tile's width = its aspect ratio × that height. A recording rides the same strip as a waveform mini-player card with a fixed aspect — so it scales with the slider exactly like a photo, and it plays in place. Drag the height slider and play a card to feel it. Every card plays the real 19-minute call; one plays at a time across the whole page.

What changed the waveform card is now a self-contained mini-player

  • Voice-memo title + ∿ icon label — removed. The waveform itself reads as sound; the card carries no caption.
  • "Now playing" dock indirection — removed. The card is not a launcher: play/pause, scrub and time live right under the waveform and it plays in place.
  • The waveform flexes to fill the tile, so there is no empty vertical space at tall row heights (the old card's one real con).
  • Transcript opens only in a split view — the transcript affordance stubs that here with a toast (the split itself is prototype 04).

Mixed gallery — photos + waveform mini-players

Chosen

Photos and recordings read as one family of tiles in a single clean horizontal scroll. Each audio card is fully live: click its waveform to scrub, hit play to hear the real call, read current / total time in mono underneath. Starting one card pauses every other on the page. The transcript icon opens the split view (stubbed here).

Rejected alternatives (earlier A / B / C explorations)
A — compact bar tile
The literal reading of spec §1: drop the canonical compact bar in a fixed-width cell. Rejected: a 44px bar floats in a sea of empty cell at tall row heights, and a fixed-width block ignores the justification math. The chosen card fixes this by letting the waveform flex to fill.
B — full-width band
Audio leaves the strip into its own full-width band between image runs. Rejected: chops the gallery into stacked sections — no single horizontal scroll — and is heavyweight for a short memo among many photos.
C (original) — card → "Now playing" dock
The card was a launcher that loaded the bar into a dock below the strip. Rejected by the owner: the dock indirection and the title/icon label were cut. The card now IS the player and plays in place — this page is the improved C.

Spec tension worth flagging

Rule §1 "one bar, everywhere" vs. the justified aspect-ratio strip. §1 says a gallery shows the same .audio-bar with only .is-compact toggled — a fixed-height horizontal control. But the strip sizes every tile by aspect × row-height, and a horizontal bar has no aspect, so it either floats in an over-tall cell or must leave the strip. The improved card resolves this by being a vertical player composed from the same kit parts — the real .audio-wave + scrub engine, the real .audio-play, the real mono .audio-time — just stacked (waveform over controls) instead of in a row. So the family holds at the component level; the layout adapts to the medium.

Minor open point — where the scrub lives. I kept the scrub on the waveform (click / drag anywhere on it, the canonical behaviour via bindScrub) rather than adding a second thin seek track in the control row, which would duplicate the playhead. The waveform is the scrub; play/pause and time sit underneath. Flag if you'd rather see an explicit seek line in the control row.
