Accio — when a hit comes from a recording

A note can match because of words spoken in its audio. Type a real word from the call — problém, partnerku, kouč, dotazník, čas, divadlo. The transcript hit shows a quiet "in recording" mark, the spoken snippet with a timecode, and each line jumps straight to that moment. A plain text note is shown alongside for contrast. ↑↓ move · Enter open · Esc clear.

Normal hit — title/body match, plain snippet, no glyph. Transcript hit — ∿ "in recording · N matches", timecoded jump lines. One accent — the mark is accent-at-low-alpha; the recording cue is icon + muted text, never a 2nd hue.
The jump is real: clicking a line (or Enter on it) seeks audio.mp3 to that second and plays — in the app this opens the transcript split view at that word.
